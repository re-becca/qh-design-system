Get help now

In an emergency

For life threatening emergency services, call Triple Zero 000

For TTY emergency service, dial 106. For more information visit the National Relay Service (NRS).

For Marine Rescue Queensland, radio VHF16 in an emergency.

For non-life threatening flood and storm emergency services

Call Queensland State Emergency Service (SES) 132 500

Watch for the warnings

The new Australian Warning System (AWS) means every warning has an icon and colour that tells you what the hazard is and what the danger level is. Each level has a set of action statements to give you clear advice about what to do.
